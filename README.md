a. The Problem
Financial institutions hold billions in unclaimed surplus funds, including auto repossession overages and escrow refunds. When a repossessed vehicle sells for more than the loan balance, the surplus legally belongs to the consumer. However, banks rely on opaque processes and bureaucratic friction, knowing that financially vulnerable consumers rarely navigate the complex claims process. This results in billions of dollars in "breakage"—unclaimed funds absorbed by Wall Street while consumers suffer.

b. The Solution
ReclaimAI is an automated, AI-driven asset recovery platform. We use proprietary machine learning algorithms to cross-reference public auto auction databases, historical loan defaults, and institutional escrow records. Our system proactively identifies unclaimed surplus funds owed to consumers and automates the entire legal and administrative claims process. We return lost capital directly to the consumer with zero upfront friction, transforming a predatory blind spot into a seamless financial recovery experience.

c. Market Opportunity
The broader U.S. unclaimed property market exceeds $60 billion and grows annually. The specific niche of auto finance surplus, escrow breakage, and institutional overpayments represents a highly fragmented $4 billion+ addressable market. With rising consumer debt and economic pressure, there is massive, untapped demand for automated financial recovery. By capturing just 2% of this highly fragmented niche, ReclaimAI unlocks a highly lucrative, recession-resistant revenue stream.

d. Business Model
We operate on a transparent, performance-based contingency model. ReclaimAI charges a standardized, legally compliant 15% success fee only when funds are successfully recovered and disbursed to the consumer. There are zero upfront costs. This asset-light, high-margin SaaS-plus-services model ensures our incentives are perfectly aligned with consumer success while generating scalable, recurring revenue as our AI processes claims autonomously.

e. Competitive Advantage
Unlike manual "finders" or legacy unclaimed property aggregators, ReclaimAI utilizes predictive AI to identify and file claims before funds escheat to the state. Our proprietary algorithm matches complex loan data with auction surplus records in real-time, reducing claim processing time by 80%. Furthermore, our compliance-first, transparent fee structure and automated legal workflows create a defensible moat against predatory, high-fee alternatives.
